## Full Description

### App Store (iOS)
**Trust me bro? Let's check.**

Tired of wild claims with zero sources? Mebro uses AI to fact-check anything in seconds. Just paste a claim, and get:

• **Instant verdict** - True, False, Misleading, or Unverified
• **Detailed analysis** - See the reasoning behind every fact-check
• **Credible sources** - Every verdict backed by real references
• **Shareable links** - Send receipts to settle any debate

**How it works:**
1. Paste any claim you've heard or read
2. Mebro researches it using AI
3. Get a verdict with sources
4. Share the link with anyone

**Perfect for:**
- Debunking viral social media posts
- Settling debates with friends
- Verifying news before sharing
- Research and due diligence

**Free to use:**
- 5 fact-checks per day for guests
- 15 fact-checks per day for registered users

No more "trust me bro" moments. Get the facts.

## Review Notes for Apple
This app uses AI (Google Gemini) to fact-check user-submitted claims. The AI researches claims using web sources and provides verdicts with citations. Results are for informational purposes only and we clearly state AI can make mistakes. No user-generated content is published without user action (sharing a link). Rate limits prevent abuse.
